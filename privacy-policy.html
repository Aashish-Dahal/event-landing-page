This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, how we use it, and the choices you have regarding your information.

By using Winzy, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1 Information We Collect

When you use Winzy, we may collect the following information.

Personal Information

When you create or use a Winzy account, we may collect:

  • Your name or username
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number
  • Your account information

We use this information to create and manage your account, provide our services, communicate with you when necessary, and help protect your account.

Game Activity

When you use Winzy and its games, we may collect information about your activity on the platform. This may include:

  • Games you play
  • Game results
  • Game history
  • Scores or points
  • Other activity related to your use of Winzy

This information is used to operate the games, maintain your game history, provide the features of the service, and improve the Winzy experience.

Device and Notification Information

Winzy uses Firebase services, including Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM), to provide notifications.

For this purpose, we may collect information such as a device or app installation identifier, including an FCM registration token.

This information helps us send notifications and communicate important information through the Winzy application.

We do not use this information to identify you personally beyond what is necessary to provide and maintain the service.

Location Information

Winzy may request access to your device's GPS-based location.

We use location information to understand the general geographic areas from which our users access Winzy and to better understand our user base and service usage.

Winzy does not use your location to continuously track your movements.

You can control location permissions through your device settings. If you do not provide location permission, some location-related features or functions may not be available.

Cookies and Analytics

Our website and application may use cookies and similar technologies to keep the service functioning, understand how users interact with Winzy, analyze usage, and improve our services.

Analytics information may include information about how you use the website or application, such as pages or features you interact with and general usage information.

We use this information to understand how Winzy is being used and to improve the service.

2 How We Use Information

We may use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Provide and maintain Winzy services
  • Provide access to games and related features
  • Maintain game activity and history
  • Send notifications and important service information
  • Understand where our users are generally located
  • Understand how users interact with Winzy
  • Improve our website, application, games, and services
  • Identify and fix technical problems
  • Maintain the security and reliability of the service
  • Prevent misuse of the platform
  • Communicate with users when necessary
  • Comply with applicable legal requirements

We only use information for purposes reasonably related to providing and improving Winzy and operating the service.

3 Firebase and Google Services

Winzy uses services provided by Google, including Firebase services.

Firebase may process certain information on our behalf to provide services such as notifications, analytics, application performance monitoring, authentication, or other features that we use in the application.

The information processed by these services depends on the Firebase and Google services enabled in the Winzy application.

Google and Firebase may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

4 Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share or provide access to information with service providers that help us operate Winzy. For example, we may use third-party services such as Firebase and Google services for notifications, analytics, application functionality, and technical services.

We may also disclose information when required by applicable law, legal process, or a valid request from a government or regulatory authority.

5 Payment and Financial Information

Winzy currently does not collect or process payment information, wallet information, deposits, withdrawals, or financial transaction information through the services covered by this Privacy Policy.

If these features are introduced in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain how related information is collected and handled.

6 Date of Birth and Age Information

Winzy does not currently require users to provide their date of birth or age when using the service.

If we introduce age or date-of-birth collection in the future, we will update this Privacy Policy to explain how that information is collected and used.

7 Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information we collect from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

We use appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect information while it is being transmitted and stored.

However, no online service can be guaranteed to be completely secure. While we work to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8 Data Retention

We retain information for as long as it is reasonably necessary to provide our services, maintain your account, maintain game records, improve our services, prevent misuse, resolve disputes, and meet applicable legal requirements.

When information is no longer required for these purposes, we may delete it or remove information that can identify you.

Some information may remain temporarily in backups or technical systems until those systems are updated or the information is automatically removed.

9 Account and Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your Winzy account and associated personal information.

To request account deletion, contact us at:

Email: winzyapp9@gmail.com

When we receive an account deletion request, we will review and process the request.

Where deletion is permitted and required, we will delete the personal information associated with your account.

Winzy will not retain an account simply by disabling or freezing it when a valid deletion request requires the associated data to be deleted.

10 Children's Privacy

Winzy is not intended for children who are below the minimum age required to use the service under applicable law.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children who are not permitted to use Winzy.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us at winzyapp9@gmail.com so that we can review the situation.

11 Your Privacy Choices

You can control certain permissions through your device settings.

For example, you can manage whether Winzy has access to your device's location or notifications through your Android settings.

You may also request deletion of your account and associated personal information by contacting us.

Depending on applicable law, you may also have rights to request access to or correction of your personal information.

12 Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

Changes may be made when we add new features, change the way our services work, introduce new third-party services, or when legal or regulatory requirements change.

When we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

We recommend checking this page occasionally for the latest information about our privacy practices.
