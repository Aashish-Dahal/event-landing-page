System 01 · Live draw

Every winner, drawn live.

When the draw goes live, a countdown holds the room. Then the machine runs with no result until the winner is drawn, and lands on that Participant ID in front of everyone. The ID is matched to the participant, checked against the campaign rules, and the prize is awarded, one tier at a time.

    How Winzy works

    Two systems. Six steps each.

    1. 01Choose an eventThe occasion the campaign runs under: a festival, a launch or an expo.
    2. 02Create a campaignDetails, prizes, dates and eligibility rules.
    3. 03Set participationRequired: Bill Scan, Guess & Win, Quiz, Yes/No, Poll or Points.
    4. 04Users participateEach one gets a unique Participant ID.
    5. 05Live drawGlass bowl, spinner or slot machine draws an ID, live.
    6. 06Win the prizeThe ID is matched, checked and awarded by the campaign rules.
    1. 01Choose a gameJhandi Burja, Spin & Win or Slot Machine.
    2. 02Open a game campaignRewards, winning conditions, number of plays, daily rewards.
    3. 03Users pick a campaignThey browse the brands' game campaigns and choose one.
    4. 04Live room or self playJhandi Burja plays live with everyone; Spin & Win and Slot Machine you play yourself.
    5. 05Play the gameOne spin or pull; Try again gives an extra one.
    6. 06Win the rewardPrizes and daily rewards, as the brand set them.
